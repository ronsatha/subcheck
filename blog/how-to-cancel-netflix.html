How to cancel Netflix (2026): every way, step by step

Cancelling Netflix takes about two minutes and there are no cancellation fees. The only real trap is where you signed up: if Apple, Google Play, Roku, or a carrier bills you, the cancel button lives with them, not with Netflix.

Before you cancel

A few things worth knowing first, because they change what you should do:

  • You keep watching until the end of your billing period. Cancelling today costs the same as cancelling on the last day, so there is no benefit to waiting, and waiting is how people get charged for one more month.
  • Downloaded titles stop playing once the account closes. Finish anything you have downloaded before your final day.
  • Someone else uses your account? Netflix lets you transfer a profile to another account first (Account, then Profiles, then Profile Transfer), so that person keeps their viewing history and My List.
  • Only taking a break? Netflix offers a pause on the cancel page for many members. See the pause section below before you cancel outright.

Cancel Netflix on the web

This is the main route, for anyone Netflix bills directly:

  1. Go to the Manage your membership page at netflix.com/cancelplan and sign in if you are asked. You can also click your profile icon on netflix.com, choose Account, and open the Membership section.
  2. Select Cancel.
  3. Under Cancel, select Finish Cancellation.
  4. Check your inbox for the confirmation email Netflix sends to the address on the account.

Netflix is blunt about one point on its help page: this is the only way to cancel your account and end your membership. Signing out of your account or deleting the Netflix app does not cancel anything.

Cancel Netflix on your phone or tablet

There is no separate in-app cancellation flow. On a phone or tablet, open a browser and follow the web steps above at netflix.com/cancelplan. Inside the Netflix app, tapping My Netflix, then the menu icon, then Account takes you to the same account page, where you select Cancel membership and then Finish Cancellation. If the app hands you over to your browser partway through, that is normal.

No cancel button? Someone else bills you

Netflix's help page says it directly: if you do not see the cancellation option in your account, you will need to cancel the account with your payment partner. The Membership section of your Account page names that partner and usually links to their instructions. The common routes:

  • Apple: on an iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, tap Netflix, then tap Cancel Subscription.
  • Google Play: open the Play Store, tap your profile icon, tap Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, tap Netflix, then Cancel subscription.
  • Roku: cancel from your account at my.roku.com under Subscriptions, or highlight the Netflix channel on your Roku device, press the Star button, and choose Manage subscription, then Cancel.
  • A carrier or TV package (for example T-Mobile or Xfinity): cancel or remove the Netflix add-on through that provider. If you later want Netflix back, sign up directly so the billing is in one place.

Pause instead of cancelling

If you just want a month or two off, the Cancel Membership page offers many members a pause instead: pause for 1 month, and you can extend it up to 3 months in total. You keep watching until your next billing date, the pause starts then, and billing resumes automatically when the pause ends. The pause is not offered on every plan or payment method, so if you do not see it, cancelling and restarting later works just as well, since your details are kept for a while after the account closes.

What happens after you cancel

  • You keep full access until the end of your current billing period. The account then closes on its own, and you will not be charged again unless you restart.
  • Netflix does not refund partial months. Because you keep access to the end of the period, there is nothing to refund.
  • If your account is on hold when you cancel, it closes immediately instead of running to the end of the period.
  • If you pay with a gift card or promotional balance, you keep using Netflix until that balance runs out, and the account closes then.
  • Netflix keeps your viewing activity, recommendations, ratings, account details, gameplay history, and game saves for 24 months after the account closes. Restart within that window and your profiles, My List, and history come back the way you left them.
  • Extra member slots you added end with the plan, so anyone you added loses access on the same day you do.

How to confirm the cancellation worked

Three checks, in order. First, the confirmation email from Netflix; keep it. Second, open your Account page and look at Billing details, which shows the date your account will close. Third, glance at your next card statement: no new Netflix charge should appear after that date. If a charge does show up, the usual cause is that someone with access restarted the account. Cancel again from the Manage your membership page, then change your password and choose the option to sign out of all devices.
