Privacy

Last updated November 3, 2026.

Your subscription list stays in your browser

SubCheck is a calculator, not an account. The subscriptions you enter are stored only in your browser, using local storage. They are not sent to a SubCheck server, because there is no SubCheck server that collects them. We never see your list. Clearing your browser data, or using Start Over on the results page, removes it from this browser.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for, and do not collect, your name, email address, payment details, bank logins, or passwords. There is no signup. The prices shown as quick-add presets are checked against each service's published pricing (last checked November 2026). Edit them to match your actual bills.

Third party services on this site

This site loads a small number of third party services. They have their own privacy policies, and they may set cookies or receive technical information such as your browser type and IP address when a page loads:

  • Google Analytics, for anonymous, aggregated usage statistics.
  • Google Fonts, for the Inter typeface.
  • Tailwind CSS and Chart.js, loaded from public content delivery networks, for styling and for the spending chart on the results page.
  • An Impact site verification tag on the homepage. There are currently no affiliate links on the site.

Cookies

SubCheck itself does not set tracking cookies for your subscription data. Analytics and the third party services above may set their own cookies. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, and the calculator will still work.

Who runs SubCheck

SubCheck is made and run by Ron Satha, an independent developer in Colorado, not a company with a data business. There is no team collecting your information and nothing to sell it to. The site code is public on GitHub, so the claims on this page can be checked rather than trusted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here, with a new last updated date.
