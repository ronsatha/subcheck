How it works

Three steps, about two minutes.

1

List your subscriptions

Type each service, its cost, and how often you are billed, or import a spreadsheet and match the columns. Add renewal dates if you know them, and tag each plan personal, business or shared.

2

See the honest total

Monthly and yearly totals, a breakdown by category, subtotals whose bill each plan is, and a nudge where two services may be doing the same job.

3

Decide what stays

Tick services in the what-if simulator to see what cancelling would save, export renewal reminders to your calendar so the next charge is never a surprise, then save or share your summary.

Common questions

Is my list sent to a server?

No. Everything you type stays in your browser, in local storage on your own device. There is no SubCheck server collecting subscription lists, and we never see yours.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no signup, no email address to hand over, and nothing to install. Open the calculator, add your subscriptions, and see your total.

How is the monthly cost calculated for quarterly and yearly bills?

Quarterly charges are divided by 3 and yearly charges are divided by 12, so every subscription is compared on the same monthly basis. Your yearly total is the monthly total times 12.

What happens to my data if I clear my browser?

It is gone. Your list lives only in your browser's local storage, so clearing your browser data, or using Start Over on the results page, removes it completely. Download the CSV first if you want a copy.
