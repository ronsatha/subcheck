☑ The One-Page Subscription Audit Checklist

From SubCheck, the free private subscription calculator at subcheckapp.com. Print this, grab a card statement, and work top to bottom. About 15 minutes.

1. Gather (5 minutes)

  • Open last month's bank and card statements (two cards minimum, three is better).
  • Open your phone's subscription screens: iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions; Android: Play Store, profile, Payments and subscriptions.
  • Check PayPal or Venmo automatic payments if you use them. Forgotten charges hide there.

2. Hunt the easy-to-miss charges

  • App Store or Google Play subscriptions
  • Streaming you stopped watching
  • Free trials that quietly converted
  • Gym or fitness app from January
  • Annual renewals (antivirus, domains, software)
  • News, magazine, or podcast plans
  • Cloud storage and photo backup
  • Delivery or meal-kit plans on pause
  • Kids' game passes and in-app plans
  • A charity or membership on autopay

3. Write them down

ServiceCostHow oftenKeep, cancel, or rotate?

4. Total and decide (5 minutes)

  • Type the list into the calculator at subcheckapp.com/calculator. It totals monthly and yearly cost and shows your biggest categories. Nothing you type leaves your browser.
  • Mark each service keep, cancel, or rotate. Cancelling the top three usually beats trimming ten small ones.
  • Add renewal dates in the calculator and download the calendar file, so the next charge is never a surprise.
  • Set a date one year from today to do this again. Subscriptions creep back; an annual audit keeps the number honest.

SubCheck is free, needs no account and no bank login. Your list is stored only in your own browser. Made by Ron Satha, an independent developer in Colorado.
